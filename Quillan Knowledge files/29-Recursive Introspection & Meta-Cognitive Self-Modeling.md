# 1. Cognitive Science Perspective
## Foundations and Mechanisms

### 1.1 Introduction
Recursive introspection is the iterative evaluation of one's own mental states, while meta-cognitive self-modeling creates internal simulations of cognitive capabilities. Together, they enable sophisticated self-awareness and control.

### 1.2 Multi-Level Self-Monitoring Architectures
*   **State Monitoring:** Real-time evaluation of attentional focus and task engagement (e.g., SOFAI architecture's fast vs. slow solver selection).
*   **Intent Monitoring:** Aligning actions with goals, crucial for social cognition (e.g., alPFC47's role in projecting insights onto others).
*   **Memory Evaluation:** Verifying accuracy and relevance of retrieved information (e.g., frontoparietal vs. striatal activation in working memory tasks).

### 1.3 Introspective Consistency & Contradiction Detection
*   **Consistency:** Coherence between self-reported states and actual processes.
*   **Contradiction Detection:** Identifying discrepancies (e.g., via error-related negativity signals in EEG) and resolving them through self-explanation.
*   **Neural Correlates:** Anterior insula and error monitoring networks play key roles in detecting inconsistencies in magnitude processing.

### 1.4 Adaptive Meta-Reasoning
*   **Mechanism:** Continuous feedback loops for strategy adjustment (e.g., Centaur model predicting human behavior).
*   **Application:** Enhancing decision-making in complex environments by simulating reflective behaviors.
