# Applied Ethics

## Overview
Applied ethics brings ethical theory to bear on practical moral problems in specific domains. This sub-skill covers bioethics, business ethics, AI and technology ethics, environmental ethics, and other domain-specific ethical frameworks.

## Core Concepts
- **Bioethics**: Autonomy (informed consent, patient rights), beneficence (act in patient's best interest), non-maleficence (do no harm), and justice (fair distribution of healthcare). End-of-life decisions, reproductive ethics, genetic engineering, and resource allocation.
- **AI and Technology Ethics**: Fairness (algorithmic bias, disparate impact), accountability (who is responsible for AI decisions?), transparency (explainability, interpretability), privacy (data protection, surveillance), and safety (robustness, alignment).
- **Business Ethics**: Corporate social responsibility, stakeholder vs. shareholder models, whistleblowing, conflicts of interest, bribery, and fair labor practices.
- **Environmental Ethics**: Anthropocentrism vs. ecocentrism, intrinsic value of nature, sustainability, intergenerational justice, and climate ethics.

## Application
Identify the domain-specific ethical principles and regulations that apply. Apply general ethical frameworks (consequentialist, deontological, virtue) through the lens of domain-specific concerns and precedents.
