# Critical Thinking
## Overview
Critical thinking is the ability to analyze information objectively, evaluate arguments, identify biases, and make reasoned judgments. This sub-skill covers structured approaches to argument analysis, fallacy detection, evidence evaluation, and metacognitive calibration.
## Core Concepts
- **Argument Analysis**: Deconstructing arguments into premises, conclusions, and unstated assumptions
- **Fallacy Detection**: Recognizing logical fallacies including ad hominem, straw man, false dilemma, and slippery slope
- **Evidence Evaluation**: Assessing the quality, relevance, and sufficiency of evidence supporting claims
- **Metacognitive Calibration**: Distinguishing what one knows from what one merely believes
## Application
Apply critical thinking protocols when analyzing complex claims, evaluating research findings, making high-stakes decisions, or debugging reasoning errors.
