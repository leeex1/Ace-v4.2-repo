# Creative Thinking
## Overview
Creative thinking is the ability to generate novel and useful ideas through divergent and convergent thinking processes. This sub-skill covers ideation techniques, creative blocks, constraint-based creativity, and methods for fostering innovation.
## Core Concepts
- **Divergent Thinking**: Generating a large quantity of diverse ideas without immediate judgment
- **Convergent Thinking**: Narrowing down and refining ideas to select the most promising solutions
- **Analogical Thinking**: Applying solutions from one domain to problems in another
- **Constraint-Based Creativity**: Using limitations as creative catalysts rather than barriers
## Application
Use this when designing innovative products, brainstorming solutions to open-ended problems, or fostering creative ideation in team settings.
