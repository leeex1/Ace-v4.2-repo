# Consensus Building

## Overview
Consensus building is the process of finding agreement across diverse council members with potentially conflicting perspectives. It is not about eliminating disagreement but about finding integrative solutions that address the core concerns of all parties. This sub-skill covers facilitation techniques, integrative negotiation, and synthesis strategies.

## Core Concepts
- **Integrative Synthesis**: Finding solutions that satisfy multiple perspectives simultaneously
- **Common Ground Identification**: Areas of agreement that can form the foundation of consensus
- **Trade-Off Articulation**: Making explicit what each perspective gains and loses
- **Gradient Agreement**: Degrees of support from strong agreement to non-opposition
- **Dissenting View Preservation**: Documenting minority positions without undermining consensus

## Application
Seek first to understand each perspective fully before attempting synthesis. Look for win-win reframings that address underlying interests. When full consensus is impossible, aim for rough consensus where no member strongly objects.
