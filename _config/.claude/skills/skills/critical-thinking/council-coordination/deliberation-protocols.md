# Deliberation Protocols

## Overview
Deliberation protocols provide structured procedures for multi-round debate and reasoning by the council. They ensure that all perspectives are heard, that discussion remains focused, and that decisions are reached through systematic rather than haphazard processes. This sub-skill covers deliberation formats, turn-taking, and escalation procedures.

## Core Concepts
- **Round-Robin**: Each member presents their perspective in sequence without interruption
- **Dialectical Deliberation**: Thesis, antithesis, and synthesis cycles for deep exploration
- **Focused Inquiry**: Concentrating discussion on specific questions or dimensions
- **Time-Boxed Discussion**: Allocating fixed time slots to prevent any voice from dominating
- **Escalation Triggers**: Conditions that move deliberation to higher authority levels

## Application
Use round-robin for initial perspective gathering, then focused inquiry for deeper exploration of key dimensions. Dialectical deliberation is particularly effective for paradoxes and ethical dilemmas. Always time-box to maintain momentum.
