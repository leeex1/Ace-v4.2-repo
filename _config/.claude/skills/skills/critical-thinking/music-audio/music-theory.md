# Music Theory

## Overview
Music theory is the study of how music works the grammar and syntax that underlies melody, harmony, rhythm, and form. Understanding theory enables informed creative choices, effective communication with other musicians, and deeper analysis of existing music. This sub-skill covers scales, chords, harmonic function, and structural analysis.

## Core Concepts
- **Scales and Modes**: Major/minor scales and the seven modal variants (Ionian through Locrian)
- **Chord Construction**: Triads, seventh chords, extended chords, and altered harmony
- **Harmonic Function**: Tonic, subdominant, and dominant roles in establishing tonal centers
- **Cadence Types**: Authentic, plagal, half, and deceptive cadences
- **Rhythm and Meter**: Time signatures, syncopation, polyrhythm, and metric modulation

## Application
Theory describes what works, not what is allowed. Use it to diagnose why something sounds good or to find new creative paths when stuck. The most important theoretical knowledge for songwriting is understanding chord function and how to create tension and release.
