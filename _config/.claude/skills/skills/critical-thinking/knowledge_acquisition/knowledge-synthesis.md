# Knowledge Synthesis
## Overview
Knowledge synthesis combines information from multiple sources to create new understanding, identify patterns, and generate insights beyond any individual source. This sub-skill covers integrative analysis, meta-analysis, conceptual frameworks, and theory building.
## Core Concepts
- **Integrative Analysis**: Combining findings from diverse sources to identify convergent and divergent results
- **Conceptual Frameworks**: Organizing knowledge into structured models that reveal relationships
- **Meta-Analysis**: Statistical synthesis of quantitative findings across multiple studies
- **Theory Building**: Developing explanatory frameworks that account for observed patterns
## Application
Apply knowledge synthesis when conducting literature reviews, developing research proposals, building domain expertise, or creating comprehensive understanding from disparate information.
