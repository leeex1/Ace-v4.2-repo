# Ethical Decision Frameworks

## Overview
Ethical decision frameworks provide structured, step-by-step protocols for analyzing ethical questions and arriving at defensible decisions. This sub-skill covers general-purpose frameworks and domain-specific protocols for ethical reasoning.

## Core Concepts
- **The Five-Step Framework**: (1) Identify the problem and stakeholders; (2) Gather facts and identify relevant principles; (3) List and evaluate alternatives from multiple ethical perspectives; (4) Make a decision and justify it; (5) Implement and monitor.
- **The PLUS Framework**: Policies (is it consistent with organizational policies?), Legal (is it acceptable under law?), Universal (does it conform to universal principles?), Self (does it match personal values?). A practical ethics check.
- **The Potter Box**: (1) Define the situation; (2) Identify values; (3) Apply ethical principles; (4) Choose loyalties (to whom is loyalty owed?). Used in communication and journalism ethics.
- **Ethical Matrix**: A structured table mapping stakeholders against ethical principles (wellbeing, autonomy, justice) to systematically evaluate each stakeholder-principle combination.

## Application
Select a framework appropriate to the decision context. Apply it systematically, documenting each step. Use frameworks not as rigid checklists but as structured guides for thorough ethical deliberation.
