# Metacognition
## Overview
Metacognition refers to thinking about thinking -- the ability to monitor, evaluate, and regulate ones own cognitive processes. This sub-skill covers the theoretical foundations of metacognition, its developmental trajectory, and its critical role in learning, problem-solving, and autonomous system control.
## Core Concepts
- **Metacognitive Knowledge**: Declarative knowledge about cognitive strategies, tasks, and ones own capabilities
- **Metacognitive Regulation**: Active control of cognitive processes through planning, monitoring, and evaluation
- **Feeling of Knowing**: The subjective sense of having knowledge that is not currently accessible
- **Calibration**: The alignment between confidence judgments and actual performance accuracy
## Application
Apply metacognitive frameworks when designing adaptive learning systems, building self-monitoring AI architectures, or developing strategies for improving human learning and decision-making.
