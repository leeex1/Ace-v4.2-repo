# Perspective-Taking

## Overview
Perspective-taking is the ability to adopt another's point of view across multiple domains: visual (what does the other see?), epistemic (what does the other know?), motivational (what does the other want?), and cultural (how would someone from a different background interpret this?). It is a core component of cognitive empathy and social competence.

## Core Concepts
- **Visual Perspective-Taking**: Level 1 (seeing vs. not seeing), Level 2 (how something appears from their angle)
- **Epistemic Perspective-Taking**: Distinguishing between what one knows oneself versus what another knows
- **Motivational Perspective-Taking**: Understanding another's goals, desires, and priorities
- **Cultural Perspective-Taking**: Recognizing how cultural background shapes interpretation and values

## Application
Use perspective-taking for designing AI that communicates effectively with diverse users, virtual reality experiences, collaborative robots, educational systems, and cross-cultural communication tools.
