# Social Cognition

## Overview
Social cognition encompasses the full range of cognitive processes involved in perceiving, interpreting, and responding to social information. It includes person perception (forming impressions), attribution (explaining behavior), stereotype and prejudice processes, social learning, group dynamics understanding, and cultural cognition. Social cognition integrates theory of mind with broader social knowledge.

## Core Concepts
- **Person Perception**: Forming impressions from thin slices of behavior; primacy and recency effects
- **Attribution Theory**: Situational vs. dispositional attributions; fundamental attribution error
- **Stereotype and Prejudice**: Cognitive shortcuts in social categorization; their origins and mitigation
- **Social Learning**: Learning from observing others' behavior and outcomes (observational learning)

## Application
Use social cognition for designing AI that makes appropriate social judgments, understanding group dynamics, reducing bias in AI systems, social simulation, and human behavior modeling.
