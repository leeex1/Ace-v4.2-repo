# Self-Directed Learning
## Overview
Self-directed learning is the process where individuals take initiative and responsibility for their own learning, including diagnosing needs, formulating goals, identifying resources, and evaluating outcomes. This sub-skill covers strategies for autonomous learning and personal knowledge management.
## Core Concepts
- **Learning Contracts**: Formal agreements between learner and mentor specifying goals, methods, and evaluation criteria
- **Personal Learning Plans**: Structured roadmaps for acquiring knowledge or skills over time
- **Resource Identification**: Finding and evaluating learning materials including courses and communities
- **Self-Motivation**: Maintaining momentum through intrinsic motivation and structured accountability
## Application
Use self-directed learning strategies when pursuing independent study, professional development, or any scenario where formal instruction is unavailable.
