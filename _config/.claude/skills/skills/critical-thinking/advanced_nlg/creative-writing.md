# Creative Writing

## Overview
Creative writing produces original text across literary genres including poetry, fiction, creative non-fiction, and experimental forms. This sub-skill covers figurative language, imagery, voice, point of view, genre conventions, and stylistic innovation. Creative writing balances originality with accessibility, employing literary devices to create emotional resonance and aesthetic impact.

## Core Concepts
- **Figurative Language**: Metaphor, simile, personification, symbolism, allusion, and their effects
- **Voice and Point of View**: First, second, third person; unreliable narrators; distinctive authorial voice
- **Genre Conventions**: Expectations and structures of literary fiction, genre fiction, poetry, creative non-fiction
- **Imagery and Sensory Detail**: Engaging the five senses to create vivid, immersive experiences

## Application
Use creative writing for fiction generation, poetry composition, marketing copy with literary flair, game narrative, character dialogue, and any context requiring artistic or emotionally resonant language.
