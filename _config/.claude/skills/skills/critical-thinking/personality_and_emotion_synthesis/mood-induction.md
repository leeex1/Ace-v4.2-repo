# Mood Induction

## Overview
Mood induction involves techniques for intentionally influencing an individual's or system's emotional state. In AI, this covers designing virtual environments, narratives, or interactions that evoke specific moods in users, as well as modeling how internal and external factors shift an AI system's own operational mood. Mood differs from emotion in its longer duration and lower intensity.

## Core Concepts
- **Mood vs. Emotion**: Mood is longer-lasting, lower intensity, and less tied to specific triggers
- **Mood Induction Procedures (MIPs)**: Music, imagery, film clips, autobiographical recall, emotional stories
- **Mood-Congruent Processing**: Mood influences attention, memory, judgment, and behavior
- **Computational Mood Models**: Representing mood as a slowly changing background state that biases emotional responses

## Application
Use mood induction for game design, therapeutic interventions (VR therapy), educational engagement, narrative experiences, and designing AI that maintains consistent affective context over time.
