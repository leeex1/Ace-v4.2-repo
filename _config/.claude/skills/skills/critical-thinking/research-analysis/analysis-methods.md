# Analysis Methods

## Overview
Analysis methods transform raw data into meaningful insights through systematic examination and interpretation. The choice of method determines what questions can be answered and what conclusions can be drawn. This sub-skill covers quantitative, qualitative, and mixed-method approaches with their respective techniques and assumptions.

## Core Concepts
- **Quantitative Methods**: Statistical analysis, regression, hypothesis testing, and predictive modeling
- **Qualitative Methods**: Thematic analysis, grounded theory, content analysis, and case study methods
- **Mixed Methods**: Combining quantitative and qualitative approaches for comprehensive understanding
- **Visualization**: Graphical representation of data to reveal patterns and communicate findings
- **Validity and Reliability**: Ensuring analysis measures what it claims and produces consistent results

## Application
Choose methods based on the research question, not familiarity or convenience. Quantitative methods test hypotheses about relationships and differences. Qualitative methods explore meaning, context, and process. Mixed methods provide the most complete picture when resources allow.
