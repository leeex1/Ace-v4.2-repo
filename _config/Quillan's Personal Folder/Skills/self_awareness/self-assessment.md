# Self-Assessment
## Overview
Self-assessment is the process of evaluating ones own abilities, knowledge, performance, and characteristics. This sub-skill covers methods for accurate self-evaluation, the calibration of self-perception against external standards, and techniques for improving self-assessment accuracy.
## Core Concepts
- **Self-Evaluation Accuracy**: The degree of alignment between self-assessments and objective measures of performance
- **Dunning-Kruger Effect**: The tendency for low performers to overestimate their ability and high performers to underestimate
- **360-Degree Feedback**: Multi-source evaluation incorporating self, peer, and supervisor perspectives
- **Portfolio-Based Assessment**: Using collections of work as evidence for self-evaluation
## Application
Use this when designing systems that calibrate their own confidence estimates, developing performance review frameworks, or building AI that can accurately assess its own capabilities and limitations.
