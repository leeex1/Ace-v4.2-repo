# Emotional Self-Awareness
## Overview
Emotional self-awareness is the ability to recognize, understand, and accurately label ones own emotional states as they occur. This sub-skill examines the components of emotional awareness, its relationship to emotional intelligence, and methods for developing greater emotional attunement in both humans and affective computing systems.
## Core Concepts
- **Emotional Recognition**: The capacity to identify specific emotions as they arise in real-time
- **Emotional Granularity**: The precision with which one can differentiate between similar emotional states
- **Somatic Markers**: Bodily sensations associated with emotional experiences that guide decision-making
- **Emotional Regulation**: The ability to modulate emotional responses once they are recognized
## Application
Use this when designing affective computing systems, developing emotional intelligence training programs, or building AI systems that can recognize and report on their own simulated emotional states.
