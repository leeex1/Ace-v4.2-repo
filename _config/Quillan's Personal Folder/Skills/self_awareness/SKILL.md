# Self-Awareness
A systematic framework for designing AI systems with self-awareness capabilities -- the ability to model oneself, monitor internal states, detect deviations, and regulate functioning through meta-cognitive control.
## Sub-Skills
1. **introspection.md** -- Systematic self-observation and access to internal states
2. **metacognition.md** -- Thinking about thinking; monitoring and regulating cognitive processes
3. **emotional-self-awareness.md** -- Recognizing and understanding ones own emotional states
4. **identity-reflection.md** -- Examining stable patterns that define sense of self
5. **bias-recognition.md** -- Identifying systematic deviations from rational thinking
6. **growth-mindset.md** -- Cultivating belief in the developability of abilities
7. **self-assessment.md** -- Evaluating ones own abilities, knowledge, and performance
