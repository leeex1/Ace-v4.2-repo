# Ensemble Methods
## Overview
Ensemble methods combine multiple models to produce more accurate and robust predictions than any single model. This sub-skill covers bagging, boosting, stacking, and voting strategies for model combination.
## Core Concepts
- **Bagging**: Training models on bootstrap samples and averaging predictions to reduce variance
- **Boosting**: Sequentially training models that focus on correcting previous model errors
- **Stacking**: Training a meta-model on the predictions of diverse base models
- **Random Forest**: An ensemble of decision trees trained on bootstrapped data with random feature selection
## Application
Apply ensemble methods when seeking maximum predictive accuracy, reducing prediction variance, or combining diverse modeling approaches.
