# Contingency Planning

## Overview
Contingency planning prepares for uncertainty by identifying what could go wrong and developing alternative paths before they are needed. It transforms brittle plans into adaptive strategies that can respond to changing conditions. This sub-skill covers risk assessment, conditional branching, and plan repair strategies.

## Core Concepts
- **Risk Identification**: Systematic enumeration of what could go wrong and its impact
- **Conditional Planning**: If-then-else branches based on sensing outcomes or milestones
- **Plan Monitoring**: Checkpoint conditions that trigger re-evaluation
- **Plan Repair**: Local modifications to an existing plan when unexpected events occur
- **Robustness Analysis**: Stress-testing plans against distribution shifts and worst-case scenarios

## Application
Identify the top 3-5 risks that would most impact plan success and develop specific contingencies for each. Place monitoring checkpoints at critical path junctions. The goal is not to predict everything but to have a response ready when the unexpected happens.
