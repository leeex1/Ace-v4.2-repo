# Qualia Theory
## Overview
Qualia theory examines the subjective, first-person character of experience -- the what it feels like aspect of consciousness. This sub-skill explores the philosophical and empirical dimensions of qualia, including the explanatory gap between physical processes and subjective experience.
## Core Concepts
- **Explanatory Gap**: The chasm between physical accounts of brain processes and the subjective character of experience
- **Inverted Qualia**: Thought experiment where two individuals have swapped color experiences despite identical behavioral responses
- **Mary Room**: Knowledge argument showing complete physical knowledge does not entail knowledge of subjective experience
- **Phenomenal Character**: The qualitative properties that distinguish conscious experiences from one another
## Application
Apply qualia theory when designing systems meant to simulate or model subjective experience, when analyzing the limits of functionalist accounts of consciousness, or when discussing the hard problem in philosophical contexts.
