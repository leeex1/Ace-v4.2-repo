# Artificial Consciousness
## Overview
Artificial consciousness explores whether and how machines could possess conscious awareness. This sub-skill examines computational approaches to modeling consciousness, including architectures based on global workspace theory, integrated information theory, and higher-order representation.
## Core Concepts
- **Functional Consciousness**: Consciousness as a functional property that could be implemented in multiple substrates
- **Computational Correlates**: Information-processing signatures that might indicate conscious awareness in machines
- **Embodiment and Consciousness**: The role of physical interaction with an environment in grounding conscious experience
- **Consciousness Metrics**: Proposed measures for detecting or quantifying consciousness in artificial systems
## Application
Apply this when designing architectures for machine consciousness, evaluating claims about AI sentience, or exploring the ethical implications of conscious artificial systems.
