# Phenomenal Consciousness
## Overview
Phenomenal consciousness refers to the experiential, qualitative aspect of mental life -- the raw felt character of sensations, emotions, and thoughts. This sub-skill examines the structure of phenomenal experience, including its temporal dynamics, sensory modalities, and affective dimensions.
## Core Concepts
- **Phenomenal Field**: The unified, integrated structure of conscious experience at any given moment
- **Phenomenal Self**: The sense of being a unified subject of experience across changing mental states
- **Phenomenal Contrast**: Differences between conscious and unconscious processing of similar content
- **Temporal Consciousness**: The structure of experienced time including duration, succession, and the specious present
## Application
Apply this when modeling the structure of subjective experience, analyzing altered states of consciousness, or designing systems that aim to capture the qualitative richness of human awareness.
