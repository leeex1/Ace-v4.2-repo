# Audio Analysis

## Overview
Audio analysis systematically examines audio recordings to extract technical, structural, and artistic insights. It bridges critical listening and data-driven understanding, enabling informed production decisions and deeper appreciation of musical works. This sub-skill covers spectral analysis, structural analysis, and production technique identification.

## Core Concepts
- **Spectral Analysis**: Visualizing frequency content and identifying sonic characteristics
- **Structural Analysis**: Mapping song sections, transitions, and energy arcs
- **Production Technique ID**: Identifying effects, processing, and recording methods used
- **Comparative Analysis**: Side-by-side comparison of mixes, masters, or performances
- **Quality Assessment**: Evaluating technical quality (dynamic range, frequency balance, artifacts)

## Application
Use spectral analysis to identify frequency buildup, masking, and balance issues. Structural analysis reveals the arrangement architecture learning from great songs. Comparative analysis helps calibrate your ears to professional standards.
