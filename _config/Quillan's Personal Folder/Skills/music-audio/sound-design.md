# Sound Design

## Overview
Sound design creates and manipulates audio elements for music, film, games, and interactive media. It is the art of sculpting sound to evoke emotion, convey information, and build immersive worlds. This sub-skill covers synthesis methods, sampling, processing chains, and psychoacoustic principles for effective sonic storytelling.

## Core Concepts
- **Synthesis Methods**: Subtractive, FM, wavetable, granular, additive, and physical modeling
- **Sampling and Manipulation**: Chopping, time-stretching, pitch-shifting, and resampling
- **Effects Processing**: Reverb, delay, modulation, distortion, and spatial effects
- **Layering**: Combining multiple sounds to create richer, more complex timbres
- **Psychoacoustics**: How human perception shapes effective sound design choices

## Application
Start with the emotional target what should the listener feel? Choose synthesis method based on the sound character needed. Layer sounds in frequency bands to avoid masking. Use psychoacoustic principles like the Haas effect and frequency masking to create depth and clarity.
