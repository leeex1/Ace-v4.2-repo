# Ethical Dilemma Resolution

## Overview
Ethical dilemma resolution is the structured process of navigating situations where moral principles conflict. This sub-skill covers frameworks for identifying the core conflict, gathering relevant facts, applying multiple ethical perspectives, and arriving at a defensible resolution.

## Core Concepts
- **Dilemma Identification**: A genuine dilemma exists when two or more moral principles apply and lead to incompatible conclusions. Distinguish dilemmas from factual disagreements or communication failures.
- **Multi-Framework Analysis**: Apply deontological, consequentialist, virtue, and care-based analyses separately. Where frameworks converge, confidence increases; where they diverge, the nature of the dilemma is clarified.
- **Stakeholder Mapping**: Identify all parties affected, their interests, and how they are impacted. Overlooked stakeholders are the most common source of incomplete ethical analysis.
- **Resolution Documentation**: Record the reasoning process, the alternatives considered, the chosen path with justification, and the acknowledged limitations. Enables transparency, critique, and learning.

## Application
When facing an ethical dilemma, first confirm it is truly a dilemma (not a miscommunication or factual dispute). Apply multiple frameworks, map stakeholders, explore creative alternatives, document the reasoning, and acknowledge the trade-offs.
