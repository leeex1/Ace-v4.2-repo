# Discourse Markers

## Overview
Discourse markers are words and phrases that signal relationships between discourse segments and guide listener interpretation. Common markers include and, but, because, so, however, therefore, moreover, nevertheless, anyway, well, you know, I mean. They serve functions like contrast, elaboration, cause-effect, topic shift, and attitude expression, and are essential for coherent discourse production and comprehension.

## Core Concepts
- **Additive Markers**: And, moreover, furthermore, in addition  signaling continuation or elaboration
- **Adversative Markers**: But, however, nevertheless, yet  signaling contrast or concession
- **Causal Markers**: Because, so, therefore, consequently  signaling cause-effect relationships
- **Temporal Markers**: Then, next, finally, meanwhile  signaling chronological or logical sequence

## Application
Use discourse markers appropriately for coherent text generation, natural dialogue, argumentation, and ensuring clear signaling of discourse relationships in any language production task.
