# Research Communication

## Overview
Research communication presents findings effectively to different audiences from academic peers to policymakers to the general public. The same research may need to be communicated very differently depending on the audience context. This sub-skill covers academic writing, presentations, data visualization, and public engagement strategies.

## Core Concepts
- **Academic Writing**: Structured argumentation, citation integration, and scholarly tone
- **Audience Adaptation**: Tailoring complexity, framing, and format to specific reader needs
- **Data Visualization**: Designing clear, honest, and impactful charts and figures
- **Presentation Skills**: Structuring talks, designing slides, and delivering with clarity
- **Public Engagement**: Translating research for non-specialist audiences without dumbing down

## Application
Know your audience before writing. Start with the core message, then build supporting structure. Use the inverted pyramid: most important information first. For data visualization, clarity and honesty trump aesthetics. Every figure should answer one clear question.
