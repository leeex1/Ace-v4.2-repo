# Depth Perception
## Overview
Depth perception is the ability to perceive the world in three dimensions and judge distances. This sub-skill covers monocular and binocular cues, stereopsis, motion parallax, and the computational approaches used to recover depth from visual input.
## Core Concepts
- **Binocular Disparity**: The difference in images between the two eyes that provides depth information
- **Monocular Cues**: Depth indicators available to a single eye including perspective and texture gradients
- **Motion Parallax**: The relative motion of objects at different distances when the observer moves
- **Stereopsis**: The brains computation of depth from the slight differences between left and right retinal images
## Application
Use this when designing stereo vision systems for robotics, 3D reconstruction pipelines, virtual reality environments, or augmented reality applications.
