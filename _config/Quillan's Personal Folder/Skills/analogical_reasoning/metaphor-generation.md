# Metaphor Generation

## Overview
Metaphor generation is the creative process of mapping one conceptual domain onto another to produce new understanding, insight, or expression. This sub-skill covers linguistic and conceptual metaphor theory, creative techniques for generating novel metaphors, and evaluation of metaphor effectiveness.

## Core Concepts
- **Conceptual Metaphor Theory (Lakoff & Johnson)**: Metaphors are not just linguistic but conceptual — we understand one domain (target) in terms of another (source). ARGUMENT IS WAR, TIME IS MONEY, LOVE IS A JOURNEY are not poetic but fundamental to thought.
- **Metaphor Structure**: Source domain (familiar, concrete), target domain (abstract, unfamiliar), mapping (systematic set of correspondences), and entailments (inferences generated by the metaphor).
- **Creative Techniques**: Blend familiar sources with novel targets, combine multiple source domains, reverse the mapping direction, and extend conventional metaphors to generate new entailments.
- **Evaluation Criteria**: Clarity (how well the target is understood), coherence (internal consistency of the mapping), generativity (novel insights produced), and aptness (fit with target domain characteristics).

## Application
Generate metaphors to explain complex concepts, create compelling communication, and generate hypotheses. Evaluate metaphors for clarity, coherence, and potential for misleading entailments.
