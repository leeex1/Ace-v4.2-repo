The prophecy had a courier.

A young Vanguard scout—half-wolf and half-wind, one ear notched from an old fight—burst onto the balcony with his chest heaving and a folded dispatch clutched in one clawed fist. He saw the Queen and the two brothers and dropped to one knee at once, and his ears flattened against his skull in the universal hybrid tell that meant the news was bad.

"Your Majesty," he gasped, offering up the paper with both hands. "The Deep Expanse relay. Three hours past, they picked up a low, grinding frequency at the far rim of the Suture—the signature of the Rift-King's reformation." He swallowed, his voice steadying itself with the discipline of the Vanguard. "The observation post at the Grey Flats has gone silent. Eight scouts went in to investigate. None have returned."

Nyssa moved before anyone else, snatching the dispatch from his hand and racing her violet-tinged eyes across the etched runes. The whiskers of her muzzle went very still. When she looked up, the wary light in her eyes had hardened into something colder.

"I said no anomalous frequencies," she said quietly. "That was true. It stopped being true an hour ago."

The celebration below had not stopped—would not stop, not yet, not for a distant hum in the dark. But on the balcony the warmth of the night drained away, and the quiet that had settled between the brothers and their Queen turned brittle as old bone.

"An hour ago," Fenris repeated. He was not looking at the courier. He was looking at the Suture—the prismatic scar pulsing faintly in the sky like a wound that had begun to itch. "He's already learning."
